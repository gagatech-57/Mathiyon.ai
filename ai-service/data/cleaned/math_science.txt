Mathematics is the study of numbers, quantities, shapes, formulas, and structural patterns.
Algebra deals with mathematical symbols and the rules for manipulating these symbols in equations.
Calculus is the mathematical study of continuous change, focusing on derivatives and integrals.
Physics explores the fundamental laws governing matter, energy, space, time, and forces.
Einstein's theory of relativity established E = mc^2, equating mass and energy.
Computer science studies algorithmic processes, computational complexity, and information systems.
Neural networks simulate biological neuron activations to approximate complex non-linear functions.
Probability theory calculates the likelihood of events occurring based on frequency distributions.
Linear algebra studies vector spaces, matrices, linear transformations, and eigenvalue decompositions.
Optimization algorithms seek the global minimum of loss functions using gradient vector updates.
